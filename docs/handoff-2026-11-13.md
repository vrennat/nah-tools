## What the merged code does

- **Spam gate on every destination:** short links, dynamic QR codes, bulk links, bio page links.
- **Daily model-call limit (#64):** 5,000 a day by default (`LINK_GATE_DAILY_MODEL_CALLS`), counted in D1. At the limit every create and destination change gets 503 before the creator's allowance is charged.
- **Shares (#68):**
  - each network (IPv4 address or IPv6 /64) gets 60 calls a day (`LINK_GATE_NETWORK_DAILY_MODEL_CALLS`);
  - each IPv6 /48 gets 600 for all its networks together (`LINK_GATE_BLOCK_DAILY_MODEL_CALLS`);
  - over a share: 429.
- **Recheck (#63):** a link whose check could not finish gets one background recheck. Only a spam verdict disables it; a recheck refused by the limit or a share leaves it active and logged. Rechecks count toward the limit and the shares. A disabled link's destination cannot be changed: its owner gets 409 before anything is charged.

## Known gaps

- An attacker with several IPv6 /48s, a /32, or a large IPv4 pool can still use up the daily limit (9 /48s or 84 IPv4 addresses reach 5,000).
- IPv4-embedded IPv6 addresses (for example `64:ff9b::1.2.3.4`) all share one /64 and one /48 key.
- A recheck that starts just after 00:00 UTC is counted under the previous day's network hashes.
